Our pipeline has four stages.

\paragraph{Backbone and data.}
We analyse ViT-B/16 \cite{Dosovitskiy2021} (12 transformer blocks, $d{=}768$, trained on ImageNet-1k) using 1,000 validation images paired with an 18-concept CLIP vocabulary spanning object categories (\textit{animal}, \textit{bird}, \textit{plant}, \ldots), textures (\textit{fur texture}, \textit{feather texture}, \ldots), and geometric patterns (\textit{spotted}, \textit{honeycomb}, \textit{scale}, \ldots).

\paragraph{Activation extraction.}
Forward hooks collect MLP output activations at Layer~6 (mid-network) and Layer~11 (late-network). Each image yields a $196{\times}768$ patch-token matrix per layer (\texttt{[CLS]} excluded), centred via a learned decoder bias following~\cite{Bricken2023}.

\paragraph{Sparse Autoencoder.}
Given activation $\mathbf{x}\!\in\!\mathbb{R}^d$, the SAE produces a sparse code
\begin{equation}
  \mathbf{z} = \text{ReLU}(\mathbf{W}_\text{enc}\mathbf{x} + \mathbf{b}_\text{enc}),\quad \mathbf{z}\!\in\!\mathbb{R}^m,
\end{equation}
and reconstructs $\hat{\mathbf{x}} = \mathbf{W}_\text{dec}\mathbf{z}$ (unit-norm columns, $m{=}8d{=}6144$). Training minimises
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L} = \|\mathbf{x}-\hat{\mathbf{x}}\|_2^2 + \lambda\|\mathbf{z}\|_1
\end{equation}
(Adam, lr$\!=\!10^{-3}$, $\lambda\!=\!10^{-3}$, batch 256; 5 epochs for Layer~6, 15 for Layer~11).

\paragraph{CLIP labelling.}
The $K{=}5$ highest-activating crops per feature are matched to the vocabulary via mean cosine similarity to \textit{``a photo of \{concept\}''} prompts \cite{Radford2021}. A feature is characterised as monosemantic when the CLIP-assigned label is confirmed by visual inspection of the top-activating crops.

\paragraph{Causal verification.}
Feature $k$ is ablated by subtracting its contribution from patch-token activations: $\mathbf{x}' = \mathbf{x} - f_k(\mathbf{x})\,\mathbf{W}_\text{dec}[:,k]$. Causal impact is quantified via the Relative Logit Drop (RLD):
\begin{equation}
  \text{RLD} = \frac{L_\text{baseline} - L_\text{ablated}}{|L_\text{baseline}|}.
\end{equation}
A dose-response sweep over ablation strength $s\!\in\![0,1]$ and a steering experiment ($s{=}5$, amplifying the feature's contribution) together rule out artefactual interpretations of the causal signal.
All causal metrics are evaluated on a single representative validation image; cross-image robustness is left as future work.
