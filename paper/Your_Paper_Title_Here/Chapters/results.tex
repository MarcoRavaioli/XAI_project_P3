\subsection{Discussion}

The results address both research gaps. SAEs recover structured, labellable features from purely visual MLP activations: all features at both depths receive distinct CLIP labels, confirmed by visual inspection. Human assessment independently validated CLIP labels for texture-dominant features; convergence on Feature~5065 (\textit{honeycomb pattern})---from both CLIP scoring and independent human review, combined with the 13.49\% logit drop---constitutes the strongest evidence of monosemantic, causally relevant behaviour. The depth-wise shift from texture detectors to semantic features reproduces the representational hierarchy expected from prior ViT analyses \cite{Raghu2021}. The concentration of causal impact on a single late-network feature is the most unexpected finding; it may reflect specialisation in the \texttt{[CLS]} accumulation mechanism, though a larger image sample is needed to confirm whether this pattern is input-specific or systematic.
